# Support

## Something is broken

Open a [bug report](../../issues/new/choose).

The most useful thing you can include is the **device and OS version** — iPhone
15 Pro on iOS 26.1, Apple TV 4K on tvOS 26.2, and so on. Most reports turn out
to hinge on a device-specific detail, and having it up front saves a round trip.

If it is a visual or timing problem, a screen recording is worth more than a
description.

## Something is missing

Feature ideas are welcome in the [issue tracker](../../issues/new/choose) too.

## The iPhone will not find my Apple TV

The controller feature needs both devices on the same network:

1. Open Neon Snare on the Apple TV first — it only advertises while the app is
   running.
2. Check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. A guest network, or a phone on
   cellular, will not find the TV.
3. On the iPhone, check **Settings → Neon Snare → Local Network** is on. If you
   declined the prompt, this is where to turn it back on.
4. Some routers block devices from seeing each other ("AP isolation" or "client
   isolation"). If everything else looks right, that setting is worth checking.
